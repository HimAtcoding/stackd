## 4. Making Stackd the seller

Apple's rules (developer.apple.com/support/enrollment, checked 2026-10-28): "To have your organization's name appear as the seller, your organization must be recognized as a legal entity and you must be enrolled as an organization." DBAs, fictitious businesses and trade names aren't accepted, so "Stackd" alone can't be the seller unless a registered entity is literally named that (for example "Stackd LLC", which would show as "Stackd LLC"). The enrolling person needs legal authority to bind the entity. The entity needs a D-U-N-S Number (free from Dun & Bradstreet), a public website on a domain associated with it, and a work email on that domain. Apple may ask for notarized business documents.

**Blocker for both paths: there's no registered legal entity on record.** Team `4JNCSTKTHQ` is Shikhar's Individual membership (renews 2027-06-17). Forming an entity (and deciding who owns it among the four) is a legal step outside this repo.

### Path A: convert the existing membership to Organization

1. Form the legal entity (LLC or corporation) and get its exact legal name.
2. Look up or request its D-U-N-S Number with the legal name and address matching the formation documents exactly (D&B's lookup tool linked from developer.apple.com/support/D-U-N-S). A new number can take days to weeks from D&B.
3. Set up a public website on a domain the entity owns, and a work email on that domain. usestackd.app could serve if the entity owns it; the support address joinstackd@gmail.com isn't on a company domain.
4. Shikhar (the Account Holder) asks Apple to convert: developer.apple.com/contact → Membership and Account → request to change from Individual to Organization, giving the legal entity name, D-U-N-S Number, address and phone. Apple's enrollment page says only "please contact us" for this.
5. Apple verifies (calls the contact, may ask for documents). After approval the seller on the App Store becomes the entity's legal name.

**Effect:** the Team ID `4JNCSTKTHQ` stays, so Sign in with Apple identifiers, signing, the bundle ID and existing users are unaffected. **But every app in this membership changes seller at once**, including Shikhar's other apps on this team (Aro, CalcBlast, MDP, PlayerVision and any others), which would all show the Stackd entity as seller. That's only right if the entity owns all of them.

### Path B: transfer only Stackd to a separate Organization account

1. Form the entity and get the D-U-N-S Number (steps A1 to A3).
2. Enroll a **new** Apple Developer Program membership as an Organization for the entity (developer.apple.com/programs/enroll, signed in with the Apple Account of the person who can bind the entity), $99 a year. Wait for verification.
3. In the new account, accept the latest Free Apps agreement (Business → Agreements).
4. Wait until Stackd meets Apple's transfer criteria (App Store Connect Help → App transfer criteria). **Today it doesn't:** the app must have at least one version released on the App Store (none is yet), and no version can be Waiting for Review, In Review, Accepted, Pending Developer Release or Pending Apple Release (1.2 is Waiting for Review). Both accounts must have accepted the latest agreements and not be pending or changing.
5. Prepare: turn off TestFlight for every build (remove all builds and testers, including Internal team and Stackd Transfer Beta and its public link, and clear Test Information), remove any Xcode Cloud data, back up the app's metadata. The Sign in with Apple Service ID `app.stackd.transfer.signin` transfers with the app.
6. **Sign in with Apple users (the biggest risk).** Apple's user identifiers (`sub`) are per team, so Supabase's stored Apple identities would stop matching after the transfer. Before transferring, generate a transfer identifier for every Apple-linked user through Apple's REST endpoint for this (Apple technote TN3159; `appleid.apple.com/auth/usermigrationinfo`, signed with the current team's key `B5ZPA5GGWL`). After the transfer, the new team exchanges each transfer identifier for the new `sub` (and relay email) within Apple's 60-day window, and the Supabase `auth.identities` rows are updated. The Supabase Apple provider settings also change to the new team ID, key and client secret. Needs code and a database change, done on a branch with a PR.
7. Shikhar (Account Holder) starts the transfer: App Store Connect → the app → App Information → Transfer App, enters the new account's Account Holder Apple Account and Team ID. The new Account Holder accepts it in App Store Connect before the request expires.
8. After acceptance, the new team re-signs builds (new certificates and profiles, `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM` in `project.pbxproj`, App ID prefix), and future releases come from that account. The bundle ID, reviews, ratings and user updates carry over.

**Effect:** only Stackd's seller changes; Shikhar's other apps stay under his name.

**Recommendation:** Path B, once there's an entity and 1.2 (or a later version) is released with nothing in review. Path A is simpler but changes the seller on every app in the team.
